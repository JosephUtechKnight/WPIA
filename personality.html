My Personality

Top 3 Traits / Hobbies

Outside of school, I play rugby league in Division 1 here in Jamaica, I make music, and I play video games. Each of these feeds into how I handle school in its own way. Rugby keeps me disciplined and training on a schedule whether I feel like it or not is basically the same skill as sitting down to finish an assignment before a deadline. Music gives me a way to switch off and reset when coursework gets heavy, so I come back to it clearer headed instead of burnt out. Gaming has probably done the most for my problem solving through a lot of games are just pattern recognition and trial and error under pressure, which isn't that different from troubleshooting a broken network config in a lab.

A Person I Look Up To

Honestly, I don't have a single mentor or hero I model myself after I look up to myself. I believe in my own ability to push through whatever's in front of me, whether that's a rugby match, a tough module, or building something new. That self belief is something I've built up over time, through sticking with things like rugby, music, gaming, school even when they got hard.

AI: Tool, Not Crutch

To me, AI is not something that does everything for me. This semester, the way I've actually used it is to get a concept explained again when I didn't fully understand it in class for example, having something re-explained in simpler terms until it clicked, rather than just moving on confused. That feels like the right use of the tool: it fills in a gap in my understanding, but the actual work, the coding, the writing, the decisions, still has to come from me. If I let it do the thinking instead of the explaining, I'd be using it as a crutch, and I wouldn't actually be learning anything in this programme.
