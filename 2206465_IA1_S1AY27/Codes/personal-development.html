Personal Development

My reflection based on "The Richest Man in Babylon" by George S. Clason, on the following chapters:

  • 7 Cures for a Lean Purse
  • 5 Laws of Gold

Reflection: 7 Cures for a Lean Purse

  • The first cure is to start thy purse to fattening. This means saving at least 10% of everything you earn instead of spending all your money.
  • The second cure is to control thy expenditures. This is knowing the difference between your needs and wants and avoiding unnecessary spending.
  • The third cure is to make thy gold multiply. This means using your savings properly so that your money can grow over time through good investments.
  • The fourth cure is to guard thy treasures from loss. This means being careful with your money and avoid investments that could cause you to lose your savings.
  • The fifth cure is to make thy dwelling a profitable investment. This teaches the importance of using your money to create a stable and comfortable place to live.
  • The sixth cure is to insure a future income. This means preparing for the future by saving and making plans so that you will have financial support later in life.
  • The seventh cure is to increase thy ability to earn. This means improving your skills, knowledge and overall self so you can create better opportunities to earn more money.
  • The cure that stands out to me the most is making thy gold multiply. I relate to this with my own goals because I know that saving money is important, but learning how to grow it is also important. Be it is through education, work, or a good investment, I want to use my money in ways that can benefit me in the future.
  • Overall, the seven cures taught me that having more money is with how you manage the money you already have. Saving, controlling spending, investing, and improving your skills can all help build a better financial future.

Reflection: "5 Laws of Gold"

  • The first law of gold says that a person should save at least one-tenth of what they earn. This build savings and prepare for the unknown instead of spending everything.
  • The second law says that money should be put to work so that it can grow. Instead of just keeping money, a person can make proper investments that help them in the long run.
  • The third law says that money should be protected by getting advice from people who are knowledgeable and experienced with investments. This can help prevent careless decisions.
  • The fourth law warns against investing in things that you do not understand/ know. If a someone doesn’t have enough knowledge about an investment, they could make a bad one and lose their money.
  • The fifth law says that money can be lost when a person chases unrealistic profits or trusts dishonest and inexperienced people. It teaches that quick money should be approached carefully.
  • The law that stands out to me the most is the third law. As when I started earning more money, I seeked out people in my life I know have been very financially well in most of their lifetime and how I can make it grow with me
  • Overall, the five laws taught me that becoming financially successful is not just about earning money. It is also about consistently saving investing and protecting your money.
